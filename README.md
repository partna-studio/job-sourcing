# LinkedIn Job Search Auto Match

An automated job sourcing pipeline that scrapes LinkedIn job postings, matches them against your resume using AI, and saves high-scoring opportunities to Google Sheets.

## Concept

This pipeline automates the tedious process of job hunting by:

1. **Sourcing** - Automatically searches LinkedIn based on your criteria
2. **Scoring** - Uses AI to match each job against your resume
3. **Organizing** - Saves results to Google Sheets for easy review
